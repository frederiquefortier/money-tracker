# Design decisions

Screen and interaction calls, with who decided and why. The built screens are not specified here: the code is the spec. This keeps what the code cannot say, the reasons and the options turned down, so they are not proposed again. Shared conventions are in [02-screens.md](02-screens.md); structural choices are ADRs in [decisions.md](../../01_building/decisions.md).

Newest first inside each section.

## Navigation and layout

- **Événements waits** (2026-11-24). After the Comparer rounds, the author removed the whole Événements tab: the basic use case is complete without it. The dashboard is Argent only, without the view toggle. The tab is in commit 45a8260 and in improvements.md; the database functions it reads (`event_totals`, `event_category_totals`) stay.

- **Comparer answers "was this one expensive for its kind?"** (2026-11-23). Asked as a senior designer, the assistant found the old card's bars all one colour, the dashed average without a number, the total (which mostly measures length) as the default, the type hidden in a menu, the dates lost by sorting on amount, the year menu ignored, and three lines per event. Built at the author's request, on the page: per day only against the type's median, a diverging bar and percent, types as tabs, newest first. The author then put it beside Événements par jour (the slips moved under both, four in a row), showed only the year's events on Année (the assistant had made the other years paler), made the tabs colour dots as Par catégorie's, and dropped the cap of eight rows. Then the header lost the usual figure and the event count for the type's tag, the year left each row on Année, and the centre line got its label, the mean (the assistant had used the median, steadier against one exceptional event; the author asked for the mean). Last, the author rejected bars inside the rows, first from a centre line, then from zero: a bar per row is not a chart and says nothing. Chosen from three sketches (columns over time, dots over time, a table alone): a column chart in date order with the mean as a named dashed line, the rows under it keeping the figures, without the percent (author). The author then asked to compare the categories inside a trip; chosen from three sketches (stacked columns, a category menu, a category table): stacked columns by top-level category, with their names under the chart since colours alone do not name them. Declined for now: a split by category under each event (it needs per-event category totals from the server).

- **Événements is the year in review** (2026-11-23). Asked as a senior designer to fix the tab, the assistant found a calendar without money (tinted day numbers, the names in tooltips), a wall of 96 small grids on Tout, spending and events in separate tabs, a list repeating the calendar, and nothing of YR-03..05. The author's brief asked for a heat map with the events, full-screen months whose days hold the transactions, events and people, sticky months at the left, and the social figures. Built: Aperçu (the heat map's months with event stripes), four highlight cards, Comparer with a total or per-day switch and the average, then the timeline calendar, a list of active days on a phone. The author chose: the social figures in this tab (no Social tab), who owes me only (PPL-05), every month on Tout, and the export left to phase 6. Then: the highlight cards as receipt slips, everyone on an expense counting for "Avec qui j'ai dépensé", an eye to hide events so a year-long event does not cover the calendar (first built per type; the author asked for every event listed under its type, as the net worth page's "Masquer des éléments" lists its items), the event tags under each month's title removed, and the overview rebuilt as Événements par jour on Dépenses par jour's component (days in their event's colour, the day counter, half width beside the slips), and no empty month before the first on record.
- **The dashboard is the first tab, and the transactions list links back to it** (2026-11-20). The nav reads Dashboard, Transactions, Patrimoine, Budget. A list opened from a dashboard figure shows an arrow back to the dashboard above its title, on the period it came from; the link stays while filters change. The author asked for both. `/` still opens the transactions list.
- **Net worth totals without cents** (2026-11-20). The headline, its change and average, and each group's total drop the cents, as the dashboard's totals; items keep theirs, being the figures typed. The author's call, from the dashboard review.

The author asked for the chart and the list side by side on desktop with the composition over the list, the group total at the far right, a smaller hover on the sparkline, and asked whether a ⋮ menu should hold a holding's five actions and why the groups had left the subpages' look, as the archived toggle read like a group title. - **The hub reads, the subpages manage** (2026-11-17). Asked as a senior designer whether the hub and its subpages repeat information or use two patterns for one job, the assistant found: "Comptes" on a link card and "Comptes" as a group beside it with two different figures (the card counts credit cards and hidden items), each group's amount both in the composition and on the list's header, the same items in two list patterns (rows that only opened a chart on the hub, links to the form on the subpages), the hide sheet grouped by institution over a list grouped by type, debts negative on Avoirs and owed in red on the hub, the eye used for both "hide from the chart" and "hidden from new transactions", a "Patrimoine" label under the "Patrimoine" title, and range tabs inside one card though they set the whole page. The author asked for every change: the link cards keep a count and lose their total (ADR-060), the composition keeps assets against debts only, a hub row opens the item's form and its chevron its chart, the hide sheet uses the list's groups, a debt is owed in red everywhere, "hidden from new transactions" takes `circle-slash`, and the tabs sit above the dashboard.
- **The transactions list follows the net worth hub** (2026-11-17). Asked as a senior designer to compare the two pages, the assistant proposed six changes. Kept: the hub's 12-column grid (the title at the far left, the filters on 3 columns, then an empty column, the list on 7 and an empty one at the right, at the author's request), the period as tabs, the search on the tabs' row capped at 18rem, and what is pending or owed under the tabs at every width (tried on the tabs' row on desktop, then kept under them by the author), and one look for groups, the header above its own card in Inconsolata with its total, which the net worth item list now uses too, with its colour dot and the transaction amount size (the author preferred the transactions' look). Built then removed at the author's request: a headline of the result's net, spent and received (so "No summary tiles" below stands again). Declined: monthly bars for the filtered set. On phones the + keeps no side gutter: the list's end clears it, and a gutter would cost a fifth of every row.
- **One period for the whole hub** (2026-11-17). The period menu (1 to 24 mois) beside range tabs read as two controls for one idea; the author asked to unify them. The tabs now hold 1M, 3M, 6M, 1A, 3A, 5A, 10A, Tout and set both what the charts draw and every change. 24 mois and 7 ans went to keep the row on one line on a phone. Then, at the author's request, Tout shows every change per year (amount over the years, percent compounded), since a change since the start says little across eight years. In the same round the "X $ (total)" line under the net worth went (ADR-059) and the item list's groups got more space.
- **The net worth chart is the net worth alone, with what moved it** (2026-11-17, supersedes the composition below). Asked as a senior designer to review the hub and then whether it would keep the chart, the assistant said no: the stacked composition sized the axis to gross assets and debts (a span several times the net worth), so the net worth's slope was flat, its line read as an edge between areas, and one large debt shaped the whole history. Built at the author's request: the net worth line alone on its own scale (four to six round gridlines, replacing a fixed step), change bars by year (by month on short ranges) split by group, the composition today as bars in the sidebar, the range as tabs, one period for every change on the page, and list rows that open their own chart in place of ticked items replacing the composition. Given up: gross assets and debts as lines over time; they live in the composition and the bars.
- **The net worth chart is a composition you scrub** (2026-11-17). Asked to rethink the chart, the assistant proposed stacked groups with the net worth line over them, the sidebar following the pointer instead of a tooltip, and monthly gain or loss bars; the author chose it over a waterfall of why it moved and a bare line. After trying it the author kept the composition but wanted the tooltip back and the sidebar left alone by the pointer. In the same round: hiding items moved to an eye beside the page title (the filter chip was a pattern found nowhere else) and the legend stopped being a toggle. A custom range was rebuilt as the calendar's month and year lists side by side, as Safari does, then removed with Personnalisé at the author's request.
- **The net worth hub: chart left, summary and links right** (2026-11-17). The total moved to a summary card on top of the right column, with net worth, assets and debts and their change over 1 to 24 months, as the spreadsheet's three cards. Accounts count as assets and credit cards as debts in the chart. Détail became a list with sparklines, each item drawn when picked, after one line per item gave about twenty tangled lines (the assistant proposed the list; the author chose it over stacked areas and a top five). Gridlines at a fixed round step. The holdings card lost its update date. The author asked for each. Then: the summary and link cards in one sticky column; debts shown as the amount owed with ▲ ▼ changes coloured good or bad, since a green "+" on a negative debt read backwards (the assistant's proposal, the author agreed); in Détail, ticked rows replace the totals at their own scale.
- **The net worth chart sits in the total's card** (2026-11-16, superseded 2026-11-17 above). The total stays the full net worth (ADR-048); while the chart leaves items out, a quiet "Sans n éléments" figure shows under it. The change over the range sits under the total; the legend chips carry only colour and name and hide their line (the author found a change per chip did not hold up). A Totaux / Détail toggle gives one line per account and holding, as the spreadsheet had. The Comptes and Avoirs cards stack to the right of the chart card on desktop. The range is one menu (1, 3, 5, 7, 10 ans, Tout, Personnalisé), as the spreadsheet had; a custom range is months, picked as a month and a year, no days. The author chose each of these when asked.
- **Cents are smaller** (2026-11-12): in every amount drawn by `Money`, the decimal separator and the cents are 0.75em. The author asked for it, reversing "Amounts at one size". Amounts written into a sentence stay plain text.
- **One control for a short choice: ChoiceField** (2026-11-12), the segmented toggle: a store or a person, a holding's nature, a category's need or want. It replaces the radios (`RadioGroupField` is gone). Nothing chosen on a required choice turns the group red, with the message under it. The author asked for one pattern; the need-or-want label "Comme {parent} ({value})" became "Hérité ({value})" so it fits.
- **Placeholders are lighter and smaller** (2026-11-12): 60% of the muted text colour, 0.85em, in the mono font, so a suggestion never reads as a typed value. The author asked for it.
- **A notice that is not an error is orange** (2026-11-12), based on #FF9300 (a darker shade of it for the text), in the shape of the form's error message (`FormAlert` with `tone="warning"`, as wide as its text): the "n transactions sans placement" link, which has a chevron and a darker tint on hover as it is a button. The author disliked amber, then found the first orange too red and gave #FF9300.
- **No "(facultatif)" on any field** (2026-11-12). A required field has its asterisk, and the others say nothing.
- **Holdings are grouped by institution, like accounts** (2026-11-12, was by kind). Institutions are shared and the author's holdings sit in few of them. The kind is a tag on the row; a drawer's total nets debts, as a credit card does in an accounts drawer, and a debt's value shows negative. The assistant recommended it when the author asked which was better.
- **The transactions list has no holdings filter** (2026-11-12). The category filter does that job. The author's call.
- **Three tabs until budgets are built: Dashboard, Transactions, Net worth** (2026-11-25). The Budget tab only showed a coming-soon card, so the author removed it; it comes back with BUD-01.
- **Four tabs: Transactions, Net worth, Dashboard, Budget** (2026-11-12, was Comptes since 2026-11-05, superseded 2026-11-25 above). Net worth is a hub, and Comptes and Avoirs are its two subpages. Everything set up once (categories, merchants, currencies, people, events, import) sits in the Réglages menu, a sheet from the left. Tab labels are one short word; "Dashboard" stays in French because "Tableau de bord" wraps.
- **Net worth is a hub with two subpages, not one list** (2026-11-12). Accounts and holdings differ in their rows, actions and grouping, and a merged list blurs both. The hub carries the totals on its two link cards, in a layout that takes the phase 5 chart above them. The subpages show no total of their own. The author asked for it; ADR-046.
- **A page opened from a hub card has a link back to the hub** (2026-11-12). The earlier rule of no back link had no use case; this is the first. A tab or menu page has none, and sheets close instead.
- **One round + button bottom right on every screen that adds something** (2026-11-05), in place of buttons beside the title. A screen with several things to add fans them out as round icons.
- **No summary tiles on the transactions list** (2026-11-05; a result headline was built and removed 2026-11-17). Spent-this-period belongs to the dashboard.
- **Amounts at one size** (2026-11-05). TravelSpend sets cents smaller; not kept. Reversed 2026-11-12: see "Cents are smaller".
- **No helper text, legends or "(facultatif)"** (author's standing rule). If a field needs explaining, fix its label or layout. A reason something is unavailable goes in a toast on tap.
- **One column width, 48rem** (2026-11-11), for the lists and the transactions list alike (`pageColumn` in `styles.ts`). The lists were 40rem.
- **Amounts and currency codes are in Inconsolata** (2026-11-11), wherever they show: `Money`, `CurrencyCode`, the typed amounts on the receipt slip and the opening balance field. Amounts inside a translated sentence (a toast, a dialog) stay in the body face.
- **Status pills are round** (2026-11-11): `Tag` follows `EventTag`. Small inline highlights (combobox options, date segments) take the field radius.
- **Sort and expand-all on pages only**, never inside a sheet or a list over a form (2026-11-05).

## Dates, period and filters

- **The transactions period is a row of tabs** (2026-11-17): the last 1, 3 and 6 months and the last year to today (1M, 3M, 6M, 1A, as on the hub), Tout, and a calendar for any other range, which then names it. Month names and years ("Novembre", "2026") were tried first; the author found them odd and asked for rolling months, as the net worth hub. A tab is a range ending today, so the calendar shows it. The period left the filters: no sidebar row, no chip, no first-level row in the sheet, and the dates sheet has no back link. The sidebar has no "Filtres" heading and shows each filter's count only (the chips name the values); its Tout effacer shows only while something is chosen, and the chips have none beside them (the author's call, at every width). The sheets' button reads "Voir les résultats", without the count. A filter with nothing to choose from takes the tap and says why in a toast. In the accounts filter, "Sans institution" comes last. The stores filter builds its rows as they near the screen, as every long list does, in place of an "Afficher plus" button (the author spotted the odd one out).
Dates went through four rounds in November 2026. The result:

- **Every date field uses the shared react-aria field and calendar** (ADR-028 as first written, 2026-11-09): typed segments (jj/mm/aaaa in both languages) and a calendar rising from the bottom of the sheet. History: native `<input type="date">` (2026-11-05) was "not cute"; shadcn's Calendar (react-day-picker) was built and dropped the same day because it cannot jump to a month or year directly; the author then wanted one look for single dates and ranges, hence react-aria.
- **The calendar's month and year are one picker** (2026-11-17, superseding the M3 header below): the arrows step month by month, and "novembre 2026" between them opens the months and the years side by side, as iOS's date input does; a pick moves the calendar at once and the name closes it. The author asked for one control in place of two lists.
- **The calendar's header follows the M3 date picker** (2026-11-13, superseded above): one pair of arrows steps month by month, and between them the month and the year each open a list in place of the grid. The author wanted to jump straight to a month or a year. Every calendar has it, the period's included.
- **The transactions period is one range calendar and nothing else** (2026-11-10, superseded 2026-11-17 above: tabs, the calendar for any other period). No year list, month grid, tabs or ‹ › stepping: three pickers for one job. An old address carrying a month or year still reads. "Tout" (the whole history) is the default and what Effacer returns to, not a tab. A pick applies at once and the sheet stays open; the footer's "Voir N résultats" closes it.
- **The period lives in the filters' sheet** (2026-11-10, superseded 2026-11-17 above), as its first screen on phones and the first row of the desktop sidebar. One place for everything that narrows the list.
- **No scrolling row of filter bubbles** (2026-11-10): sideways scroll hides filters. Below `lg` one Filtres button lists the filters (two navigation levels). From `lg` up a sidebar left of the list (2026-11-16, after the setproduct sidebar template) shows that first level, always in view: each filter with what is chosen and Tout effacer; a row opens the sheet on that filter alone. Category and people lists are too long to unfold in a sidebar, so they stay in the sheet with its search.
- **Chips are always one per chosen value**, never merged into "Catégories · 5". A sidebar row or the Filtres button always shows its own name, with what is chosen or the count.
- **A filter with nothing to choose from is disabled**, found by a cheap existence query, not by loading its list.
- **Every sheet slides in from the right**, at every width; only the menu and the desktop filters sheet (beside the sidebar, 2026-11-16) come from the left.
- **Groups in a sheet's check lists start folded** (2026-11-16): institutions, category types, people groups and events alike, except a lone group, which starts open (author, 2026-11-19: one drawer to open is a tap for nothing). A search shows its matches as plain rows.
- Tried and rejected: a row of bubbles per filter on desktop (replaced by the sidebar, 2026-11-16); a sidebar unfolding each filter's list inline (built and dropped the same day, the lists being too long); popover per filter on desktop (the sheet already holds search, grouped lists and the count; left as an improvement), year list as check rows with a second click clearing, a twelve-year grid.

## Transactions

- **The form is one screen, not steps** (2026-11-04, after a step-by-step version was tried). A "receipt slip" with the amount, currency and category first, then the details.
- **No time of day** (2026-11-04). A transaction has a calendar date, so no time zone can move it.
- **Pending is blue and owed is amber, with no visible label** (the spreadsheet's colour for pending); a bubble names each and screen readers hear them. Pending wins on a row that is both.
- **Incomes are green, expenses the text colour, and red never marks an amount** (red means an error).
- **A transfer is one row though it is two legs**; its fees hang under it on the row's elbow line, drawn apart from the amounts.
- **Converted amounts** show the booked amount muted under the display amount. That line is the marker: no ≈, no underline.
- **A merchant's default category fills the category** unless one was chosen by hand or the transaction has splits.
- **"Magasin" in French, "merchant" in the code and docs** (2026-11-04).

## Categories

- **Free colour, quick picks, automatic variation for sub-categories** (2026-11-03). A new top-level category takes the first quick pick no sibling-level category of its kind uses. A sub-category starts from a variation of its parent's colour and follows it until a colour is picked by hand. The icon is drawn white or a dark shade of the same hue, whichever passes 3:1.
- **Need or want is three choices** (2026-11-05; radios then, a ChoiceField since 2026-11-12): "Hérité ({value})" (follows the parent, stores nothing; was "Comme {parent} ({value})"), Besoin, Envie. Tried first: a select, a pill with icons, chips, then two buttons; two buttons cannot show three states.
- **The transfer-fee category is a checkbox on one expense category** with an info popover (2026-11-05). One category holds the role; ticking it elsewhere takes it over and moves existing fees.
- **Icons are coolicons, no emoji** (ADR-020). The icon follows the name until the user picks one by hand.
- **Favourite star removed** (2026-11-04); the picker's "Fréquentes" works out frequent categories from transactions (CAT-06).
- **A category pause**: archiving can take a "Réactivée le" date, for the years a category sat unused in the spreadsheet.

## People and reimbursements

- **A group is never a line on a transaction, only its members** (2026-11-05), so each person's part and debt stay their own.
- **Proche is the word in the interface** for a person.
- **An empty amount field means not known yet** and never blocks the save (2026-11-08). The reimbursement sets an unknown part.
- **Linking a reimbursement is done from the income only** (2026-11-05). The first linked line gives the income its expense's category, as a refund.
- **Repayments above the expense are allowed** (2026-11-11). A roommate paying two months at once is not a gift; the income keeps its category and that category's total can go below zero. Rejected: refusing it in the import check, or unlinking it.
- **No notifications** (2026-11-05). The amber "me doivent" bubble and the tag on a person's row are the reminders.
- **"With me" is a role and "bought from" a field on the transaction** (2026-11-11, ADR-040), after "this person is the shop" made no sense.

## Events

- **An event has no categories** (2026-11-09). It links every expense and income inside its dates; the author removes a link by hand from a transaction that does not belong, and that removal is remembered.
- **Event types have a name and a colour, no icon** (2026-11-09). The tag is a soft tinted pill, readable on any hex.
- **Dates are optional.** With none, the event links by hand only. The `auto_link` checkbox does the same for an event with dates (2026-11-11, ADR-042).
- **Tags were taken out of the plan** (2026-11-05): see improvements.md.

## Import

- **Steps, not a files card or a drop zone only** (2026-11-10). Then, after seeing it: two steps (the check is not one), a stepper after a Figma reference, a first step like a CSV upload dialog, "Téléverser" always visible, file icons in round badges without a check mark, and a "big figures" summary with the files as one quiet line.
- **Before it is created, a new merchant, person or event can be merged, renamed (part sent to the note) or dropped** (2026-11-10). A merge keeps the old spelling as an alias.
- **Starting balances are imported**, not dropped; no row is silently discarded (2026-11-10).
- **Currencies the import names are created**, not refused.

## Accounts

- **A drawer's total is as large as a transaction's amount** (2026-11-12): `text-lg`, semibold, on the accounts and the holdings drawers and on their rows. The author asked for it; "Amounts at one size" stays for the rest.
- **Institutions are managed in Réglages, not on the accounts page** (2026-11-12). Accounts and holdings share them, and most holdings sit in other institutions than the accounts, so the accounts page showed empty groups and could offer a delete the database refused. The accounts page groups only institutions with an open account. The author asked for it; ADR-047.
- **One account per currency for multi-currency providers** (Wise CAD, Wise EUR, grouped under one institution). Each balance has its own statement and an account books in one currency (TRX-02), so balances stay exact. The `multi_currency` account type was dropped. Moving between balances is a transfer; paying in euros from CAD is an expense with an original price (CUR-08).
- **A currency's favourite star removed** (2026-11-05): a user keeps a handful, so pinning saved nothing.
- **Removing a currency never changes a transaction or account**; it leaves pickers only. An open account blocks it; the default cannot be removed. Adding it back skips the rate check (`removed_at`).
