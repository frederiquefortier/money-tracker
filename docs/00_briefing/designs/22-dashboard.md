# Dashboard

**Status:** built (Argent and Événements); Événements redesigned on 2026-11-23 as the year in review, the social figures included (no third tab). Shared rules: [02-screens.md](02-screens.md). Decision: [ADR-063](../../01_building/decisions.md).

## Purpose

The spreadsheet's dashboard, the events calendar and the yearly review on one page (author, 2026-11-18). It answers what the Aspire sheet answered: what came in, what went out, what was invested and what remains.

## Requirements

DSH-01..10, EVT-04, EVT-05, YR-01..05. Next: YR-07 (export, phase 6).

## The page

Route `/dashboard`, the Dashboard tab, full width (author, 2026-11-19: a main page). At the title's right, the period menu (Tout, the default, then each year from the newest back to the first transaction's), then Argent and Événements as two icons, like a list or grid switch (author, 2026-11-20). A year counts by month, Tout by year. The period is in the address; the view is remembered per browser. The whole history loads once, so switching the period loads nothing. A click opens the transactions list on the part clicked, never the whole chart: an earned or spent bar its month (or year) with the income or expense categories, a stacked part or a donut slice its category, a day of the heat map that day, a table row's name its category over the period; a category takes its sub-categories. Only the part under the pointer is outlined, without a band over the month. The list then shows an arrow back to the dashboard on the period it came from (the address's `dashboard`, kept while filters change).

Every figure is my share, net of repayments, in the display currency (ADR-063). A missing rate shows the missing-rate mark, never a partial sum.

### Argent

From the answer to the detail (author, 2026-11-19, after a design review), two cards side by side from `xl` (author, 2026-11-20): on Année the summary beside the cumulative chart, the monthly chart beside the days (on Tout the summary beside the yearly chart), Dépenses beside Revenus, then Par catégorie across the page; one column below, in that order. Earned is green, spent red, invested yellow, what remains pale lavender, in every card. A month of the period without any transaction (between the first month on record and the last one over) is named in a warning tag beside the page title, as on the net worth page, and shaded grey on the monthly chart. Amounts show no cents. The month (or year) not over yet is drawn lighter and named "en cours" in tooltips; a month still to come keeps its slot without a label.

Under it, Dépensé, Investi and Reste, each with its share of income and its change ("▲ 4 % vs 2025", spending up in red), the changes on one line. The net worth is not on the dashboard (author, 2026-11-19); its ×N moved to the net worth page.

- **Revenus et dépenses cumulés** (Année): "Revenus + reste 2025" (income on top of what earlier years left) against Dépenses, two plain lines that stop at this month (no filled area), and last year's two lines dashed over the whole year. - **Dépenses par jour** (Année): the year as a heat map of twelve small month calendars, six across (three on a phone) so it is no taller than the monthly chart beside it, weeks from the locale's first day, without weekday letters and without a legend. Hovering a day names it and its amount on a line whose height is kept, so the card never moves; the card's corner reads the days with spending over the days gone by, "239/324 jours" (the whole year on a past one). Loaded when the year is shown, from `my_lines` for that year (a past year once per visit).
- **Dépenses:** the period's spending split three ways (Par priorité, Par groupe, Par catégorie) and drawn two ways (Par mois: each bucket stacked; Répartition: a donut from 12 o'clock, clockwise in the list's order, beside its list with each part's share and amount, the list scrolling past a set height), the choices kept per browser. The drawing is two icons in the card's header beside the total and the filter; the total beside the title; the split is a menu (Par priorité, Par groupe, Par catégorie), as the net worth list's grouping. No legend under the stacked bars: the tooltip names each part. The filter is a chip as on the transactions list, "Filtrer", with "· 2" and the accent outline while categories are ticked. Needs and wants are two shades of the spending red, groups and categories their own colour, every one shown (author, 2026-11-20: no Autres). A category filter and the period's total.

- **Par catégorie:** the sheet's table, figures only, the category column fixed while the rest scrolls, rows without a tint; the figures right after the category and the trend last, at the right, so a phone shows this month beside the names. Only categories with a transaction in the period show. The header holds expand-all, a sort (Nom A à Z, Z à A, Couleur, Montant plus élevé or plus faible, by the period's total, siblings sorted within their group) and the type as three tabs, each a dot in its flow's colour without a label (spent red, earned green, invested yellow). The average column reads "Moyenne par mois" alone. A month without any transaction breaks the trend's line. Each group folds its categories (kept per browser), each row's trend opens its chart under the row, as an item's line on the net worth page. On Année: Ce mois-ci, Cette année and Moyenne par mois over the same months, all from the year shown: to today on the current year and to December on a past one (the month and the year then named), from the first month on record when the history starts within the year; each trend covers the same months; on Dépenses, a month already more than 10 % over its average is marked ▲ in red. On Tout: a total per year and Moyenne par année, over every year shown, this one included. On Année, after the figures, Évolution vs 2025: the year so far against the same months a year earlier, nothing without a figure a year earlier; no comparison on Tout, as the summary (author, 2026-11-20); spending up in red, income and investments up in green. On Année, an opened chart draws last year's same months as a pale grey dashed line over the bars (author, 2026-11-20). The sheet's Target and Goal columns wait for the budgets (BUD-01).

### Événements

The year in review (author, 2026-11-23, after a design review): a quick look at the events and every day's spending, the highlights, the comparison, then the calendar, the long part, last. The social figures live here; the planned Social tab is dropped. Every figure is my share in the display currency, as on Argent.

The period starts at the first month on record (author, 2026-11-23: no empty month before the data), on Tout and on that first year.

- **Événements par jour** (author, 2026-11-23: as Argent's Dépenses par jour, the same component, `DayGrid`): the period's months as small calendars, six across (three on a phone), a history starting mid-year leaving its earlier months as blank places so each year keeps its rows; the year beside January and the first month on Tout. A day with an event in its type's colour (split diagonally between several, paler when to come), a day without one in the light grey. The card's corner reads the days with an event over the days gone by ("41/327 jours"); hovering a day names it and its events on a line of kept height. A month's name scrolls the calendar to it; a day opens its transactions. From `xl` it is half the page, Comparer beside it, the four highlight slips in a row under both (author, 2026-11-23). An eye in the card's header opens "Masquer des événements", every dated event under its type (newest first, those without a type last), ticked when hidden as the net worth page's "Masquer des éléments", a type ticking all its events, kept per browser: a hidden event leaves the overview, the calendar and what names a top day, so an event of a year or more does not cover the rest; its spending stays, and the highlights' event counts and Comparer still count it (author, 2026-11-23). A day in the spending red's four steps, as on Argent; each event's days underlined in its type's colour (neutral without a type). Hovering a day names it, its amount and its events on a line whose height is kept. Clicking a month scrolls the calendar to it.
- **Faits saillants**, four receipt slips two by two beside Événements par jour (one column on a phone), as the transaction form's slip: the title in capitals, a dashed rule, then one line per row with the label in capitals and the figure at the right, a muted detail under it (author, 2026-11-23). Over the period:
  - **Jours les plus chers** (YR-03): the three costliest days, each with its date, its amount and what drove it (its event, or else its largest transaction's merchant or category); a day opens its transactions.
  - **Avec qui j'ai dépensé** (YR-04): the person I spent the most with and the one I spent the least with, each with what those shared expenses cost me. A person counts on an expense whatever their role, the seller included (author, 2026-11-23).
  - **Le plus d'événements** (YR-04): the three people on the most events of the period, with the count.
  - **Me doivent** (YR-05, PPL-05): who owes me today, whatever the period, as the people list's tag; a row opens the person. What I owe others stays out of scope (PPL-05, author, 2026-11-23).
- **Comparer** (EVT-05; author, 2026-11-23, after a design review): a sort (Date plus récente, the default, or plus ancienne; Nom A à Z or Z à A, as the events list; Par jour plus élevé or plus faible; kept per browser; author, 2026-11-23), then the types with two dated events or more as tabs, each a dot in its type's colour (on Année, only the types with an event that year). Beside the title, the type as its tag in its colour (author, 2026-11-23: no figure in the header). Then a column chart (author, 2026-11-23, after rejecting bars in each row): one column per event in date order, its cost per day cut into its top-level categories in their colours (the largest overall at the bottom), their names under the chart; the axis named by month on Année and by year under its first event on Tout; the mean (of the type's events that cost something, every year) a dashed line named "Moyenne 173 $" at its right end. Hovering a column names the event, its total per day and each category's; clicking a part opens the event's transactions in that category. Under the chart, one row per event in the sort's order: the name opening its transactions, the year (on Tout only), days and total under it, and the cost per day. On Année, only that year's events, the mean still from every year. Every event shows, without a cap (author, 2026-11-23). An event with a missing rate or that brought money in has no bar, the latter's figure in green.
- **Calendrier** (EVT-04, YR-02): a timeline with the calendar. From `lg`, the period's months as a sticky column at the left (each month's spending and its number of events, years as headings on Tout), the month on screen highlighted, a click scrolling to it. At the right, each month at least the screen's height: its name and its spending, then the month as seven columns (author, 2026-11-23: no event tags under the title, the bars name them). A day's box holds its number (opening the day's transactions), its spending, a tint in the heat map's step, its events as bars across their days, up to two transactions (merchant or category, then amount) and "+N" past them, and the avatars of the people on its expenses. Below `lg`, the months are a sticky row of chips and each month is a list of its days with activity only, each as the box's content on one row. On Tout, every month since the first transaction, each built as it nears the screen (author, 2026-11-23). Every year of the period loads with the tab, in parallel, since the highlights rank over all of it (ADR-066).
