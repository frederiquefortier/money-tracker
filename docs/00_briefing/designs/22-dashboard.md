# Dashboard

**Status:** built (Argent and Événements); the Social tab is next. Shared rules: [02-screens.md](02-screens.md). Decision: [ADR-063](../../01_building/decisions.md).

## Purpose

The spreadsheet's dashboard, the events calendar and the yearly review on one page (author, 2026-11-18). It answers what the Aspire sheet answered: what came in, what went out, what was invested and what remains.

## Requirements

DSH-01..10, EVT-04, EVT-05, YR-01. Next: YR-03, YR-04, YR-05 in the Social tab.

## The page

Route `/dashboard`, the Dashboard tab, full width (author, 2026-11-19: a main page). At the title's right, the period menu (Tout, the default, then each year from the newest back to the first transaction's), then Argent and Événements as two icons, like a list or grid switch (author, 2026-11-20). A year counts by month, Tout by year. The period is in the address; the view is remembered per browser. The whole history loads once, so switching the period loads nothing. A click opens the transactions list on the part clicked, never the whole chart: an earned or spent bar its month (or year) with the income or expense categories, a stacked part or a donut slice its category, a day of the heat map that day, a table row's name its category over the period; a category takes its sub-categories. Only the part under the pointer is outlined, without a band over the month. The list then shows an arrow back to the dashboard on the period it came from (the address's `dashboard`, kept while filters change).

Every figure is my share, net of repayments, in the display currency (ADR-063). A missing rate shows the missing-rate mark, never a partial sum.

### Argent

From the answer to the detail (author, 2026-11-19, after a design review), two cards side by side from `xl` (author, 2026-11-20): on Année the summary beside the cumulative chart, the monthly chart beside the days (on Tout the summary beside the yearly chart), Dépenses beside Revenus, then Par catégorie across the page; one column below, in that order. Earned is green, spent red, invested yellow, what remains pale lavender, in every card. A month of the period without any transaction (between the first month on record and the last one over) is named in a warning tag beside the page title, as on the net worth page, and shaded grey on the monthly chart. Amounts show no cents. The month (or year) not over yet is drawn lighter and named "en cours" in tooltips; a month still to come keeps its slot without a label.

- **Summary:** Gagné as the headline with its change against the same stretch a year earlier (the months so far on the current year, the whole year before on a past one), and income as one bar cut into Dépensé, Investi and Reste (author, 2026-11-19: the full bar reads as income). Under it, Dépensé, Investi and Reste, each with its share of income and its change ("▲ 4 % vs 2025", spending up in red), the changes on one line. At the card's foot, the four flows per month over the table's months ("Moyenne par mois · sur 11 mois"), per year on Tout. On Tout, no comparison. The net worth is not on the dashboard (author, 2026-11-19); its ×N moved to the net worth page.
- **Gagné et dépensé par mois** (par année on Tout): paired bars without a legend, the summary beside it naming the colours; a month over where spending passed income shaded red (never the month in progress), the tooltip naming the shading.
- **Revenus et dépenses cumulés** (Année): "Revenus + reste 2025" (income on top of what earlier years left) against Dépenses, two plain lines that stop at this month (no filled area), and last year's two lines dashed over the whole year. Each line is named where it ends, in place of a legend: this year's at its head, last year's by the year past December; the tooltip gives the full names ("Revenus 2025 + reste 2024", "Dépenses 2025").
- **Dépenses par jour** (Année): the year as a heat map of twelve small month calendars, six across (three on a phone) so it is no taller than the monthly chart beside it, weeks from the locale's first day, without weekday letters and without a legend. A day with spending in one of four steps of the spending red, cut at the quartiles of the year's spending days; a day without spending in the same light grey as days to come (author, 2026-11-19); a day with a missing rate a darker grey. Hovering a day names it and its amount on a line whose height is kept, so the card never moves; the card's corner reads the days with spending over the days gone by, "239/324 jours" (the whole year on a past one). Loaded when the year is shown, from `my_lines` for that year (a past year once per visit).
- **Dépenses:** the period's spending split three ways (Par priorité, Par groupe, Par catégorie) and drawn two ways (Par mois: each bucket stacked; Répartition: a donut from 12 o'clock, clockwise in the list's order, beside its list with each part's share and amount, the list scrolling past a set height), the choices kept per browser. The drawing is two icons in the card's header beside the total and the filter; the total beside the title; the split is a menu (Par priorité, Par groupe, Par catégorie), as the net worth list's grouping. No legend under the stacked bars: the tooltip names each part. The filter is a chip as on the transactions list, "Filtrer", with "· 2" and the accent outline while categories are ticked. The period's total sits beside the title, without a label. The donut's list fades at its bottom edge while more sits below, and at its top once scrolled (author, 2026-11-20). Needs and wants are two shades of the spending red, groups and categories their own colour, every one shown (author, 2026-11-20: no Autres). A category filter and the period's total.
- **Revenus:** beside Dépenses, by group, with the same Par mois or Répartition switch, filter and total.
- **Par catégorie:** the sheet's table, figures only, the category column fixed while the rest scrolls, rows without a tint; the figures right after the category and the trend last, at the right, so a phone shows this month beside the names. Only categories with a transaction in the period show. The header holds expand-all, a sort (Nom A à Z, Z à A, Couleur, Montant plus élevé or plus faible, by the period's total, siblings sorted within their group) and the type as three tabs, each a dot in its flow's colour without a label (spent red, earned green, invested yellow). The average column reads "Moyenne par mois" alone. A month without any transaction breaks the trend's line. Each group folds its categories (kept per browser), each row's trend opens its chart under the row, as an item's line on the net worth page. On Année: Ce mois-ci, Cette année and Moyenne par mois over the same months, all from the year shown: to today on the current year and to December on a past one (the month and the year then named), from the first month on record when the history starts within the year; each trend covers the same months; on Dépenses, a month already more than 10 % over its average is marked ▲ in red. On Tout: a total per year and Moyenne par année, over every year shown, this one included. On Année, after the figures, Évolution vs 2025: the year so far against the same months a year earlier, nothing without a figure a year earlier; no comparison on Tout, as the summary (author, 2026-11-20); spending up in red, income and investments up in green. On Année, an opened chart draws last year's same months as a pale grey dashed line over the bars (author, 2026-11-20). The sheet's Target and Goal columns wait for the budgets (BUD-01).
- **Par marchand** (MER-04, ADR-064): below the table, across the page. Each merchant with a transaction in the period, at my share: its name (opening its transactions over the period), its totals in the category table's columns without the average (Ce mois-ci and Cette année, or each year then Tout, the whole history, on Tout), Transactions, then Tendance, its total per month as the categories' trend in the primary purple, a month without a visit a zero on the line; no change column (author, 2026-11-20). The name and figure columns have the category table's widths, so the two tables line up; rows light on hover, a zero is muted. The header has a search on the name ("Rechercher un marchand") and the sort: Nom A à Z, Z à A, Montant plus élevé or plus faible (the period's total), Transactions plus or moins, kept per browser, with no arrow in the column heads. Rows are built as the list nears the screen, with the lists' spinner. The chevron before the name, or the trend, opens under the row, within the card's width on a phone, the bill as the transaction form's receipt slip (Moyenne as the main figure, Plus petite, Plus grosse, then Moyenne 2025 with the change on its line; the name, count and total are in the row) beside the total per month in purple, as tall as the slip, last year dashed; one row open at a time.
